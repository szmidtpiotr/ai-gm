## Overview

Every mechanical combat action (attack hit, miss, damage dealt, enemy defeated, player hurt) gets a short 1-2 sentence narrative wrapper generated by the LLM. The critical constraint: the LLM receives the mechanical outcome FIRST and narrates what happened — it never decides the outcome. This is "narration-after-resolution," not "LLM decides then we check."

---

## Design Context

### Why short text per action, not one big narrative at round end?
In TTRPG combat, each hit/miss/death has a dramatic moment. "The goblin swings — you dodge by a hair" lands differently than a paragraph summary at the end. Short, punchy text per action keeps the pacing fast and maintains tension. Round-end summaries feel like a recap, not gameplay.

### Why LLM after mechanics, not before?
If the LLM narrates before mechanics are resolved, it might say "your blade finds his heart" while the dice say the goblin is still at full HP. This is the classic hallucination problem: LLM invents the reality instead of describing it. With result-first narration, the LLM knows: player rolled 17, hit (enemy AC 12), dealt 8 damage, enemy now at 3 HP. The narrative is constrained by reality.

### Why not use a template bank instead of LLM?
Templates are faster and cheaper but repetitive. After 10 fights, "Your sword bites deep into the goblin's flesh" feels like a loading screen message. The LLM produces variety. The constraint (short, mechanics-informed, Polish) keeps it controlled.

## Full Specification

### When Narration Is Generated

One narration call per combat action, triggered AFTER mechanical resolution:

| Action | Narration trigger |
|--------|-----------------|
| Player attacks — HIT | After damage calculated |
| Player attacks — MISS | After miss determined |
| Enemy attacks player — HIT | After damage dealt to player |
| Enemy attacks player — MISS | After miss determined |
| Enemy defeated (HP → 0) | After final hit confirmed |
| Player HP → 0 (before death save) | After damage dealt |
| Player flees — SUCCESS | After flee roll resolved |
| Player flees — FAIL | After flee roll resolved |
| Player uses healing potion | After HP restored |

### Narration Input (sent to LLM)

```json
{
  "action_type": "player_attack_hit",
  "attacker": "Aldric (Warrior)",
  "target": "Goblin Scout",
  "roll": 17,
  "total": 20,
  "vs_ac": 12,
  "damage": 8,
  "target_hp_before": 11,
  "target_hp_after": 3,
  "weapon": "Sword",
  "nat_20": false,
  "language": "pl"
}
```

### LLM Narration Prompt

```
You are narrating a single combat action in a dark fantasy TTRPG.
Write EXACTLY 1-2 sentences describing what happened.
Do not add dialogue. Do not add future consequences. Describe only this moment.
Do not use the word "attack" — describe the action vividly.
The outcome is: {outcome summary}.
Tone: gritty, visceral, but not gratuitous.
Language: Polish.
```

Example prompts and expected outputs:

**Player hits goblin, deals 8 damage:**
> Input: player_attack_hit, roll 17, damage 8, goblin now at 3/11 HP
> Output: "Twój miecz tnie w ukos — goblin cofnął się z krzykiem, ściskając ramię."

**Player misses:**
> Input: player_attack_miss, roll 6, goblin AC 12
> Output: "Ostrze śmiga w powietrzu — goblin uchyla się w ostatniej chwili."

**Enemy hits player, deals 5 damage:**
> Input: enemy_attack_hit, goblin, 5 damage, player now at 6/11 HP
> Output: "Zardzewiały nóż rozrywa rękaw i wrzynający się w skórę ból mówi ci, że trafił."

**Nat 20 (player):**
> Input: player_attack_hit, nat_20=true, damage doubled
> Output: "Cios idealny — ostrze przebija tarczę i zatrzymuje się dopiero na kości. Goblin pada bez słowa."

**Enemy defeated:**
> Input: enemy_defeated, goblin, final blow by player
> Output: "Goblin osuwając się po ścianie wydaje tylko krótki świst powietrza — i milknie."

### Narration Caching / Fallback

Narration generation adds ~1 second per action. If the LLM is slow or fails:
- Use a template fallback per action type (12 templates, one per action type)
- Log the failure
- Never block combat resolution waiting for narration

Template fallback examples (Polish):
- `player_attack_hit`: "Twój cios trafia w cel."
- `player_attack_miss`: "Twój cios nie trafia."
- `enemy_attack_hit`: "Wróg trafia cię swoim atakiem."

### Nat 20 / Nat 1 Special Cases

**Nat 20:** Double damage already resolved mechanically. Narration must reflect a spectacular hit.
Context injected: `"CRITICAL HIT — describe a spectacular, decisive blow"`

**Nat 1 (player):** Auto-failure + complication. Narration must reflect something going wrong.
Context injected: `"FUMBLE — describe the attack going comically/dangerously wrong"`
The complication itself is mechanical (enemy gets advantage next round, or minor self-damage) — the narration describes it but doesn't invent it.

## Integration with Round Flow

In Task 12's action log:

```json
{
  "actor": "goblin_1",
  "action_type": "attack",
  "hit": true,
  "damage_dealt": 6,
  "narrative_text": "Zardzewiały nóż rozrywa rękaw..."
}
```

The `narrative_text` is populated AFTER mechanical resolution, BEFORE the full log is returned to the frontend. The entire round is resolved mechanically first, then narration is generated for each action, then the complete log is returned. This means the LLM makes N calls (one per action) but they can be parallelized.

---

## Edge Cases

- **Multiple enemies attack same round:** Generate narrations in parallel (async), assemble in order
- **Player uses item with no interesting mechanic:** "You drink the potion. The warmth spreads through your body." — template is fine
- **Combat ends in 1 hit (player insta-kills enemy):** Still generates narration — this is the epic moment
- **Very long combat (10+ rounds):** Narrations should avoid repetition. LLM receives last 2 narratives as context to avoid repeating the same phrasing. Not critical to implement in v1.
